Human trajectory

Grok 4.6 · 18 matched website pairs · 16-hour cap

A human exploration trace raises the mean reconstruction score from 2.8% to 9.8%, with each website weighted equally.

Exploration + implementation

Grok 4.6 · 20 reconstruction runs

Among the 17 runs that reached a first implementation edit, agents visited 124 of 127 core page families and attempted 121 of 122 workflows, but reached only 4 terminal outcomes.

Early stopping

16-hour limit · one continuation case

The paper reports a median run duration of 3.57 hours against a 16-hour limit.

In a single Kimi K3 reconstruction of Lowe’s, enabling Claude Code’s /goal review extended the run from 1.0 to 10.9 hours and raised the score from 0% to 31.3%.
